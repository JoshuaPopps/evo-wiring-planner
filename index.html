      Old chopper wisdom

      Simple enough to diagnose beside the road.

      Before first power: disconnect the ignition and lights, verify every branch for shorts to ground, then energize one fused circuit at a time. Component wire colors and terminal names vary—your exact ignition, regulator, and switch manuals always outrank this planner.

      Planning reference for custom Evo Big Twin and Evo Sportster motorcycles. Not a factory service diagram. Use automotive primary wire, proper circuit protection, strain relief, and a wiring diagram for every component you install.
